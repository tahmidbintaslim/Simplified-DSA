Privacy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Short version: Simplified DSA is free. To read the lessons you sign in with a free account. With your permission, we measure how the lessons are used so we can make them better. We never sell your data and we show no ads.

Your account

Accounts are run by Clerk, our sign-in provider. Clerk stores your email address (and your name and picture if you sign in with Google or GitHub) and keeps your session signed in with a cookie. We use your account only to let you in and to tell learners apart in our usage numbers. You can delete your account at any time from the account menu.

Usage analytics (only if you allow it)

The first time you visit, a banner asks whether we may measure usage. If you choose "Allow", we use PostHog (EU hosting) to record which pages you open, when you use a demo, your quiz answers (right or wrong) and when you mark a lesson complete. These events are linked to your account's internal ID, not your email. If you choose "No thanks", nothing is sent. You can change your mind by clearing this site's data in your browser.

What you type into a demo stays in your browser and is never sent.

Your progress

When you mark a lesson complete or answer a quiz, we save that to our database (Neon, a Postgres host) under your account's internal ID, so your progress follows you to other devices and we can see which questions confuse learners. We store which lesson, which quiz, whether the answer was right and the page language; never your email or anything you typed into a demo.

Stored in your browser

A copy of your progress, your theme choice, your language choice (a cookie named lang) and your analytics choice are kept in your browser.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel's servers see standard request data (such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested) to deliver the page and protect against abuse. See Vercel's privacy policy.

The Claude Code plugin and the MCP connector

The plugin's server runs on your own computer and sends nothing to us. The public connector at https://dsa.tahmid.space/mcp answers tool requests (such as "trace merge sort on 5, 2, 9, 1") without sign-in and does not store them. Vercel's standard request logs apply. The assistant you use has its own privacy policy.

Children

Simplified DSA is meant for learners aged 13 and older.

Your rights and contact

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you. Write to the contact address on tahmid.space. If this policy changes, this page will say so before the change takes effect.
